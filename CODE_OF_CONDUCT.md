# Code of conduct

Probarion follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
That page is the full text. In short, everyone who takes part in issues, pull requests, reviews and
the project's posts is expected to:

- be respectful, and assume good faith when you disagree;
- criticise the work, never the person;
- leave out harassment, insults, sexualised language and personal attacks, and never publish someone
  else's private information.

It applies in every project space, and wherever someone speaks for the project in public.

**To report a problem,** send a direct message to [@probarionhq](https://x.com/probarionhq) on X.
Probarion has one maintainer, who reads every report, keeps the reporter's identity private and
responds as the covenant's enforcement guidelines describe, from a private warning to a ban.
