The team's testnet vaults, read from the chain (2026-11-14, UTC)

What this records
-----------------
The figures of two rows of "Tested, in numbers" in the README (and of the card "How far it is tested" on /usage):
"Testnet team vaults, read from the chain" and the testnet refusals on chain in "Testnet runs with a person and a
model" and "Canary rehearsals". The vaults are the 9 that the factory vaults.probarion.testnet lists and
v0.probarion.testnet, all of them the team's.

How it was read
---------------
Read-only requests, with no key: nothing was signed or sent.

1. View calls on https://rpc.testnet.fastnear.com with finality final: list_vaults on vaults.probarion.testnet,
   then list_mandates, list_spends and list_anchors on each vault, 100 at a time from index 0. A vault's signed
   ledger entries are the count of its last anchor, which covers every entry up to it.
2. FastNEAR's transaction index, POST https://tx.test.fastnear.com/v0/account with
   {"account_id": "<vault>", "is_success": false}, for each of the 10 vaults.
3. Each transaction the index listed, read back with the JSON-RPC method tx on
   https://archival-rpc.testnet.fastnear.com: its signer, its method and the error the contract panicked with.

A spend the contract refuses fails its transaction and creates no spend, so list_spends does not show it; step 2
and step 3 find it.
